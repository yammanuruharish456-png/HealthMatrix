Who We Are

Pioneering Healthcare with Heart

HealthMatrix Hospital was founded in 1994 with a singular vision: provide world-class healthcare that is accessible, compassionate, and outcome-driven. Over three decades, we've grown from a 50-bed facility to a 1,200-bed tertiary care hospital.

Our multidisciplinary approach — combining diagnostic precision, advanced treatment, and patient-centered care — has made us one of the most trusted healthcare institutions in the region, serving over 2 million patients from 40+ countries.

Our Mission
Accessible, affordable, quality healthcare for all — while advancing medicine through research and education.
Our Vision
To be the most trusted healthcare provider, recognized globally for clinical excellence and innovation.
Accreditation
